# What's new in the MascotasPanel Android app

## Version 1.1
- New MascotasPanel icon.
- The new-version warning now works: when you open the app it tells you if there is a newer one and lets you download it.
- Everything new in the panel also works in the app: associations, animals up for adoption and pasting Google Maps links for dog parks.

## Version 1.0
- First version: your MascotasPanel on your phone, connected to your own server.
- Works with your domain (for example https://pets.mydomain.com) and with your home IP on wifi. If you enter both, the app uses whichever works.
- Take the photo of your pet or of a dog park with the camera, or pick it from the gallery.
- "I'm here" on the maps uses the phone's location (only if you allow it).
- The "Missing" poster is saved in Downloads/MascotasPanel.
- In Spanish and English: it uses the language you choose in the panel.
- Tells you when there is a new version of the app.
