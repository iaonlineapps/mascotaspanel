# What's new in MascotasPanel

## 1.0.14
- **Easier administration on the phone:** shortcuts at the top (👥 People, 🐾 All profiles, 🌳 Dog parks, ⚙️ Settings,
  📲 Telegram) take you straight to each part, without scrolling through the whole summary.
- The admin's Home now explains that it shows your own pets and messages; data for the whole site is in «Administration».

## 1.0.13
- **Reload inside the Android app:** pull the page down (when you are at the very top) and it reloads. There is also a
  «🔄 Reload the page» button in your account menu, shown only inside the app. No need to install a new app: it works
  with the one you already have.
- **Setting a dog park's place right:** when adding or editing one there is step-by-step help («Share» → «Copy link» in
  Google Maps). Before saving, the map zooms in on the point and asks **«Is it here?»** so you can check it.
- **«➕ Add a dog park here» from the map:** tap «Show the ones near me» (or tap the map) and, if you are at a dog park
  that isn't shown, add it from there with the place and address already filled in. Also on the public dog park map.
- **No repeated dog parks:** before saving, it checks whether there is already one within 50 m or with the same name. If
  so, it shows it and doesn't create another. If it really is different, tap «It's a different dog park» and the admin
  will check it, marked as «⚠️ Possible duplicate».

## 1.0.12
- **More privacy in the Registry:** everyone only sees their own pets (marked «✏️ Yours», they open in your panel). For
  the rest, only the totals are shown (pets, dogs, cats and lost). Lost pets are visible to everyone in «Lost» and those
  looking for a home in «Adoption». The admin sees everything.
- **🚨 «It's lost» button:** in «My pets» and at the top of each pet's page (dog, cat or any other). With one tap and a
  confirmation, it shows right away on the public website and on everyone's Home, with or without an account.
- **Remove the notice whenever you want:** «🏠 I've found it» or «↩️ It was a mistake», which deletes the notice without a trace.
- **All dog parks in your panel:** «Dog parks» now shows a map at the top with every dog park, the ones everyone can see
  (even without an account). Yours that the admin hasn't checked yet are grey, only for you, marked «Waiting for approval».
- **Fixing your dog park no longer removes it from the map:** if you edit it to move it or change the photo, it stays visible to everyone.
- **Profile photo or logo:** in «My account» you can add your photo (or your association's logo). It shows at the top, next
  to your name. It's optional: if you don't add one, the paw print 🐾 is shown.
- **Get the app from the menu:** your account menu and the top of the public website now show «📱 App (Android only)».
  The app only works on Android phones and tablets; on iPhone or iPad use the website.
- **Home with the latest sign-ups:** a strip with the names of the latest pets added («🐶 Just registered: Hans»), with no
  photo or details, and the total of dogs, cats and others.
- **Going back works:** the website pages (Registry, Lost, Dog parks, Adoption, profiles) open in the same tab, so the
  phone's Back button and the back gesture work. At the top you get «🏠 My panel» to go to your Home.
- **Fuller footer:** «Non-profit project, free and ad-free · App made with the help of artificial intelligence (AI) ·
  © 2026 IA Online Apps · Personal, non-commercial use».
- Lost and wanted animals can be seen by anyone, with or without an account, on the home page and in «Wanted».

## 1.0.11
- **Dog parks in the right place:** the map point can now be **dragged** with your finger or mouse. If you use «I am here»
  on a device without GPS (the location comes from the internet and can be many km off), it warns you in orange and
  **asks before saving**, so you can fix it by searching your town, dragging the point or pasting the Google Maps link.
- **Google Maps links:** pasting the «Share» link from Google Maps (short maps.app.goo.gl links too) reads the location
  better and it **wins over GPS**: neither the photo nor «I am here» change it without asking. If it can't be read, you
  get a clear message and the place doesn't change.
- **«I am here» fills in the details:** it adds the street, neighbourhood and town, and if OpenStreetMap has a dog park a
  few metres away it suggests it by name. It also warns if that dog park is already on the website.
- **Search a dog park by name or address:** you get a list to pick from and the map goes straight there.

## 1.0.10
- **«Show the ones near me» no longer sends you to another city without warning:** on a computer without GPS the
  location comes from the internet and can be many kilometres off. Now you see the error circle and a warning when it is
  approximate. You can also **type your town or neighbourhood** above the map, or tap **«🏠 Go to my area»** (your account's area).
- When marking a dog park or where your pet got lost with «I am here», it also warns if the location is approximate,
  so you can correct the point on the map.
- **The app is Android only, and now it says so clearly:** «About» shows «Download the app (Android only)». On iPhone
  or iPad the link is not shown: it explains how to save the website to the home screen.

## 1.0.9
- **The web icon is no longer the same as the app's:** if you save the website to your phone's home screen or your
  computer, its icon has a **«WEB»** label and is called «Mascotas Web». The Android app icon stays without a label.
  If you had already saved it, remove it and save it again to see the new icon.

## 1.0.8
- **It no longer looks like you were signed out:** when going from your Home to «Adoption», «Dog parks» or the public
  registry, the top said «Sign in» even though you were signed in. Now it says **«🐾 My panel»** and takes you back.

## 1.0.7
- **Move a profile to another account:** at the bottom of the pet profile, «Move this profile to another account». Type
  the username or email and **everything moves as it is**: the photo, the QR (the tag still works), the poster and its
  notices. The other account accepts it on its Home. If you are the administrator, it moves right away.
- **What you have to review, on your Home:** the administrator sees at the top the dog parks and associations waiting for
  approval, with their photo, and approves them with one tap (or checks them first on the map and Street View).
- **Share on Instagram and TikTok:** on your notice and on the public page of any lost or adoptable pet, a button
  downloads the image (4:5 for Instagram, vertical for TikTok) with its photo and QR, and copies the text to paste it.
  Facebook, WhatsApp, Telegram, X and email are still there, and the public page now also has «Copy text».

## 1.0.6
- **New Home screen:** when you sign in you first see the **dogs and cats people are looking for** (with photo, area
  and since when), a summary of your pets and notices, and the animals up for adoption. The sections are still at the top, on the right.
- **The administrator goes straight to the dashboard** with the numbers and charts; the sections come after.
- **Fuller privacy policy:** the privacy policy and legal notice clearly say it is a **non-profit** project (free, no ads,
  no data sold), which data is required, where it is kept and how it is protected, that no profiling is done and
  what happens with Telegram notices.

## 1.0.5
- **Check a dog park before approving it:** in Administration, tapping its name opens its card with the photo, the
  map, who added it and when, and buttons to see it in **Street View**, Google Maps, satellite and OpenStreetMap.
  Approve, edit or delete it from there. The Telegram notice also has the Street View link.

## 1.0.4
- **Share the notice on Facebook, WhatsApp, Instagram or by email:** when your pet is lost (or up for adoption) you
  get the **post already written** with a "Copy text" button, and buttons for Facebook, WhatsApp, Telegram, X and email.
  The link shows its photo on Facebook and WhatsApp. For Instagram, download the **4:5 image** with its photo and the QR.
- **The public profile can be shared too:** whoever sees it can pass it on to friends with one tap.
- **Dog parks:** whoever added one (and the administrator) can now **edit** or delete it. New ones **wait for the
  administrator's approval** before showing on the map.
- **Telegram bot for the administrator:** notices with photos of everything (sign-ups, lost and found pets,
  associations with their logo, dog parks), **buttons to approve** from the phone, /estado, /perdidas and /pendientes
  commands, and a **summary every night**. Connect it with one tap from Administration.

## 1.0.3
- **Associations and shelters:** when signing up you can choose "I'm an association or shelter". They add their logo,
  who they are and how to contact them (phone, WhatsApp, Telegram or email, only what they choose to show). The
  administrator **verifies** them before anything is published.
- **Animals up for adoption:** verified associations publish their animals with a message ("Will you adopt me?"). They
  appear in the new **"Adoption"** section, in a **carousel** on the home page, in "My pets" and in the admin dashboard.
  Anyone who wants to adopt writes to the association from the profile.
- **New look:** new logo, illustrated cover and illustrations in "Adoption", "Dog parks" and when you have no pets yet.
- **Dog parks from Google Maps:** paste a Google Maps link and the location is set automatically. The administrator
  can **import the dog parks** already on OpenStreetMap around their city.
- **More data protection (GDPR):** minimum age 14, basic privacy information when signing up, permission from the second
  contact person, a fuller privacy policy and a new **legal notice**. The administrator enters their details as data
  controller in Administration.

## 1.0.2
- **Dog parks ("Find your dog park"):** a shared map of the dog parks near you. Add one with a photo:
  the location comes from the photo itself, from "I'm here" or by tapping the map. The map is public, with
  a **"Directions"** button for Google Maps, and never says who added it. Location and camera data are
  removed from photos when they are saved.
- **Clearer "Missing" poster:** choose **"QR only"** or **"My phone"** right on the poster. If you choose
  your phone, it asks for your consent first. New **Share** button.
- **Administrator dashboard:** total profiles, accounts, lost pets and dog parks, a chart of sign-ups by
  day, week or month, latest profiles, most registered animals and recent activity ("Hans signed up",
  "Luna got lost"…).
- **Telegram notifications (optional):** the administrator gets a message when someone signs up, adds a
  pet, a pet gets lost or found, a notice arrives or a dog park is added.
- **Administrator settings kept apart** from normal account settings, in their own tab.
- **Android app** (signed APK): tells you when there is a new version and lets you choose your server.

## 1.0.1
- **Your details, hidden by default.** Nobody sees your name, email, phone or area. Whoever scans the QR
  sends you a **notice** without seeing your phone, and it arrives in the new **"Notices"** tab.
- **Phone only with your consent:** if you tick it on the profile, your phone shows on its profile and in
  big letters on the "Missing" poster, only while the pet is lost.
- **Second contact person** on each profile (your partner, your daughter…), with the same rules.
- **Protected microchip:** only stored if you consent and never shown. If you want, someone with the full
  number (a vet, for example) can find the profile.
- **Public registry of all pets**, not only lost ones: photo, name and breed, with no details about their
  owners. Each owner chooses whether their pet appears.
- **More animals:** ferret, parrot, hen, guinea pig, mouse or rat, hedgehog, reptile, fish, donkey, pig, goat…
- **Map of lost pets** and, when you mark one as lost, **where it got lost on the map** (approximate, about
  100 m), with a button to open it in **Google Maps**.
- **Poster** with "MISSING · LOST" in big letters at the top.
- **Frame the photo** when you upload it: move it and zoom with your finger or the mouse.
- **Appearance:** light or dark, 8 colours and a **background with paws, bones and more** (My account → Appearance).
- A fuller **account menu**: settings, appearance, password, switch user and sign out.
- The administrator can **edit any profile** and **download the full registry** (Excel/CSV).

## 1.0.0
- **First version.** Free pet registry: everyone signs up with a first name (no surname or ID number),
  an email, a phone number and, if they want, an approximate area.
- **Profiles with a photo** for each pet: animal, breed, colour and markings, sex, size, age, microchip,
  what it is like and health.
- **QR tag** for the collar: scanning it opens its profile with buttons to call, send a WhatsApp or send
  where it is.
- **Is it lost?** Mark it as lost and it will show on the public list of lost pets, with a search by area
  and by animal.
- **"Missing" poster** as a PDF (A4 with tear-off strips) or as an image for social media, with photo,
  details and QR.
- **Your data is yours:** download it or delete everything (account, pets and photos) with one button.
- In **Spanish and English**, with quick account switching.
